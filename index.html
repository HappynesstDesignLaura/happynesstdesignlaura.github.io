01 / ABOUT THE APP

Made for a small creative business.

Happynesst Design creates printable wall art. Happynesst Design Studio supports our internal content workflow by organizing Etsy product information, reviewing product images, and preparing Pin drafts and a publishing calendar.

The app is intended for use by our own business. Pinterest connection and automated publishing features are under development and depend on Pinterest API authorization.

01

Organize

Bring together Etsy listings and product imagery in one workspace.

02

Curate

Review images and exclude size guides and other informational graphics from promotional drafts.

03

Plan

Prepare descriptions, review Pin drafts, and organize a content calendar.
