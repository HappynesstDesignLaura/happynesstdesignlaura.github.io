Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Happynesst Design Studio (“the App”) is a desktop tool developed for Happynesst Design to manage its own Etsy artwork catalog and prepare Pinterest content. This policy describes the information the App accesses and how it is handled.

1. Who operates the App

The App is operated by Happynesst Design. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to happynesstdesign@gmail.com.

2. Information the App accesses

When authorized by the account owner, the App may access Etsy shop information, active listing details, listing titles, product images, and listing URLs to organize artwork and prepare content drafts. Etsy authentication tokens and API credentials are used to connect the App to the authorized account.

When Pinterest integration becomes available and is authorized, the App may access the Pinterest account information and boards necessary to create or manage Pins, according to the permissions granted. Pinterest publishing is not active in the current desktop preview.

3. How information is used

Information is used to display Etsy products, identify images suitable for promotional use, prepare titles and descriptions, create a draft posting schedule, and—if later enabled and authorized—publish approved content to Pinterest. The App is intended for internal use by Happynesst Design, not as a public service for other sellers.

4. Storage and security

The current desktop App stores product metadata, image selections, and scheduling information locally on the computer using a SQLite database and image cache. Etsy authorization credentials are stored using the operating system's credential storage. No online database or independent analytics service is operated by this website.

As with any locally installed application, security also depends on the device and its operating system. We do not claim that any storage method is completely risk-free.

5. Sharing and third-party services

The App communicates with Etsy to retrieve authorized listing information. If Pinterest integration is enabled in the future, it will communicate with Pinterest to perform authorized actions. We do not sell personal information. Etsy, Pinterest, and the website hosting provider may process information under their own privacy policies when their services are used.

6. Website data

This website is a static informational website. We do not intentionally place advertising cookies or run our own visitor analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website.

7. Retention and deletion

App data is stored locally until it is deleted from the device. The operator can remove local database and cached image files and revoke access through the connected platform's account settings. For help with a privacy or deletion request, email happynesstdesign@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if App functionality or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
